# About

Computer Security Research is an open-access journal publishing original work on
the security and privacy of computer systems: threat modeling, operating-system
and platform security, cryptographic engineering, forensics, and the
verifiability of devices and software.

## Aims and scope

The journal favors rigorous, vendor-neutral analysis grounded in the published
technical literature. Submissions should state their threat model and scope
explicitly and distinguish claims supported by cited evidence from claims that
remain asserted.

## Open access

All articles are published open access under a Creative Commons license and are
free to read, download, and redistribute with attribution. See the
[License](../license/) page.

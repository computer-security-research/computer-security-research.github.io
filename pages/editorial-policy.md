## Publication ethics

Authors must disclose conflicts of interest and may not submit work under review
elsewhere. Plagiarism and fabricated or falsified results are grounds for
rejection or retraction.

## Corrections and retractions

Published articles are kept at stable URLs. Substantive errors are addressed by
a dated correction notice appended to the article; articles are not silently
altered, and URLs are not changed.

Authors retain copyright and grant the journal a license to publish. Articles
